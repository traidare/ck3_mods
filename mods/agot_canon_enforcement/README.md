# AGOT: Canon Enforcement

Keeps the characters a canon storyline depends on from dying to accidents they
never chose. **AGOT: Canon Children EZ Mode** already gives canon parents a
health boost; this mod extends that same protection window to the deaths the
health system cannot prevent.

## Requirements and load order

Requires **A Game of Thrones** and **AGOT: Canon Children EZ Mode**. Load after
both.

## Who is protected

Everyone currently carrying the canon children health boost — that is, canon
parents, until five years after their last canon child is born. With the manual
marking rule enabled, a decision and an interaction protect and release any
character by hand.

## What protection means

- **Travel.** Protected characters travel with overwhelming safety, so storms at
  sea, bandits, floods, spoiled food and the other travel dangers are not drawn
  on journeys they lead.
- **Battle.** They are barred from serving as knights, which keeps them out of
  battlefield casualty rolls, and they cannot be the commander who falls.
- **Duels.** They are not offered a duel on the battlefield. They are not better
  duellists; they are simply not challenged. An optional rule makes them
  unbeatable in single combat instead.
- **Accidents.** They survive kraken attacks, drownings, failed treatments,
  tournament accidents, dragon hatchings, natural disasters and similar mishaps.

Murder, execution, poisoning, sacrifice and war deaths are untouched. A
protected character can still be killed on purpose.

Six game rules control this, one per area, so any part can be turned off. All
protection ends when the health boost does.

## Limits

A protected character riding in someone else's entourage can still be lost with
that party when a journey ends in disaster, and the guests at a failed dragon
hatching are not covered.
