# AGOT - Inner Circle Free Dismissals

Makes the **Inner Circle** dynasty legacy actually waive the prestige cost of
dismissing a court position, as its description says it does.

## Requirements and load order

Requires **A Game of Thrones**. Load this mod after it and after any other mod
that changes court position costs.

## What it changes

Inner Circle promises that house members can be dismissed from a court position
without losing prestige. In the base game and in A Game of Thrones that waiver
is written into each court position separately, and most of them never received
it — Master of Horse, Master of Hunt, Cupbearer, Court Physician, Court Tutor,
Executioner, Court Jester, Lady-in-Waiting, Food Taster, Champion, Bodyguard,
Seneschal, Travel Leader, Wet Nurse, Admiral, Household Guard, Goldcloaks and
First Sword among them. Firing a relative from any of those still cost the full
25 or 200 prestige.

With this mod, once your dynasty holds Inner Circle and a member of your house
holds a court position, court position dismissals cost no prestige. The game
cannot tell a cost lookup which holder is about to be dismissed, so the waiver
covers your whole court while a house member serves in it, rather than only the
house member's own seat. Employ no one from your house and the ordinary prestige
cost returns.

Salaries, appointment costs, AI behavior and every other part of Inner Circle
are untouched.

## Known gaps

A few positions write their dismissal cost as a fixed number instead of using
the shared cost, so they are out of reach of this mod and still charge prestige:
the Night's Watch keeper seats, the Night's Watch Septon, and the Dothraki
Bloodrider.
