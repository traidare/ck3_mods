# AGOT Debug: House Transfer

A debug and storytelling tool. Right-click any living character and move them
into your own house instantly.

## Requires

- A Game of Thrones

Load order: **A Game of Thrones** first, this mod anywhere after it.

## What it does

Adds one character interaction, **Move to My House**, under the red _Debug
Actions_ category in the right-click menu. It is visible only to the player,
only on living characters who are not already in your house, and it ignores
diplomatic range, so it works on anyone you can select.

The interaction is accepted automatically. Nobody is asked, nothing is paid, and
no opinion, prestige, legitimacy, or claim consequence is applied.

Two optional checkboxes appear when you send it:

- **Also move their descendants who share their current house** — moves their
  children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and great-great-grandchildren,
  but only those still in the house the character is leaving. Anyone who already
  branched off into another house stays where they are.
- **Also reset the coat of arms of every title they hold** — repoints each title
  they hold at your house's coat of arms.

## Things to know

- This is a cheat. It is meant for testing, for repairing a save, and for
  bending a playthrough into the story you want. It is not balanced.
- Moving a character changes their dynasty as well, because a house belongs to a
  dynasty.
- Succession, inheritance, and AGOT's legitimate-house mechanics are
  recalculated from the new house from that point on. Titles the character
  already holds keep their existing coat of arms unless you tick the second
  checkbox.
- Living characters only. The dead stay in the house they died in.
