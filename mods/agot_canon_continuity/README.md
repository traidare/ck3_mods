# AGOT: Canon Continuity

Keeps the characters a canon storyline depends on from dying to accidents they
never chose, and sees to it that the riders the histories name actually get
their dragons. **AGOT: Canon Children EZ Mode** already gives canon parents a
health boost; this mod extends that same protection window to the deaths the
health system cannot prevent.

Requires **A Game of Thrones** and **AGOT: Canon Children EZ Mode**. Load after
both.

## Who is protected

Everyone currently carrying the canon children health boost — canon parents,
until five years after their last canon child is born. With the manual marking
rule on, a **Protect from Death** interaction under DEBUG ACTIONS covers any
other character by hand, granting them that same health and plague resistance
too; **End Protection** releases them.

## What protection means

- **Travel.** Storms at sea, bandits, floods, spoiled food and the other travel
  dangers are not drawn on journeys they lead.
- **Battle.** They are barred from serving as knights, which keeps them out of
  battlefield casualty rolls, and cannot be the commander who falls.
- **Duels.** They are not challenged on the battlefield. They are not better
  duellists; an optional setting makes them unbeatable instead.
- **Accidents.** They survive kraken attacks, drownings, failed treatments,
  tournament accidents, dragon hatchings and natural disasters.
- **Plots.** Off by default. Hostile schemes against them never succeed, so a
  murder plot can run its course and still fail. The game applies this by scheme
  category, so it also stops abduction, claim throne and seize realm.

With Plots off, murder, execution, poisoning, sacrifice and war deaths are
untouched: a protected character can still be killed on purpose. Seven rules in
the **AGOT - Canon** category switch each area on and off, and all protection
ends when the health boost does.

## Canon dragon tamings

A Game of Thrones claims a canon rider's dragon on the rider's tenth birthday,
which leaves out anyone already older when they first appear — Nettles above all
— and anyone the claim never reached. A further rule fills those gaps: AI canon
riders take the dragon the histories record, on the day the histories give. No
dragon is taken from a rider who has one, no bonding attempt under way is
interrupted, and a rider whose dragon has died stays riderless. It needs A Game
of Thrones' own Canon Dragons rule enabled.

## Limits

A protected character riding in someone else's entourage can still be lost with
that party when a journey ends in disaster, and the guests at a failed dragon
hatching are not covered.
