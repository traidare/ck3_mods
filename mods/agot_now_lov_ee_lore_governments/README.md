# AGOT NOW + LoV + Essos Expanded Lore Governments

Gives Essos lore-appropriate governments, and corrects Jogos Nhai culture and
Ibben faith history, across the combined AGOT, Nobility of Westeros, Legacy of
Valyria, and Essos Expanded map.

## Requirements and load order

Load immediately after **AGOT NOW + Legacy of Valyria + Essos Expanded World
Data**, and before the generic runtime-fix and full-playset compatches.

## What it changes

Governments follow the described ruling institutions of each people:

- Dothraki and Jogos Nhai rulers are nomadic; the Dosh Khaleen are theocratic.
- The Red Priesthood is theocratic. Norvos, Lorath, and Qohor are left to A Game
  of Thrones, which covers those cities itself.
- The Free Cities use administrative or oligarchic forms.
- The Ghiscari slave cities, the Valyrian Freehold, and Qarth are oligarchic.
- Yi Ti uses celestial government for the God-Emperor and meritocratic
  government below him; Leng uses mandala government.
- Poorly described or decentralized peoples use conservative tribal, clan, or
  feudal approximations.
- Explicit pirate, ruin, wilderness, unknown, and landless governments are left
  alone rather than overwritten by geographic rules.

Ibben changes at the Doom. Before it, rulers and provinces follow the God-King
and the island is a feudal God-King realm. From the Doom onward they follow the
Sound, and the realm becomes oligarchic to represent the Shadow Council — the
God-Kings ended with the Doom, so no later God-King bookmark is offered.

It does not change landed-title structure, map data, terrain, holdings, names,
dynasties, or unrelated faith history.
